Privacy Policy

Version 2.2 — Effective August 16, 2026

wingscript ("we", "us", "our") provides a real-time conversation intelligence tool delivered as a Chrome browser extension, web application, macOS companion app, and iOS app. This policy covers those products and the wingscript.com website. It describes what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data Controller

Weavery, LLC, a California limited liability company doing business as wingscript ("wingscript"), is the data controller of the Personal Data you provide through wingscript's applications and website. For questions about how your data is handled, contact us at support@wingscript.com.

3. Other People on Your Calls

wingscript transcribes conversations, so the words of the people you talk to are part of your transcripts. Those people are not wingscript users and do not see this policy, so this allocation of responsibility matters:

  • You are responsible for consent. Laws on recording and transcribing conversations vary; some jurisdictions — including California — require the consent of all parties. By using wingscript you confirm that you have obtained any consent required by the laws that apply to your conversations.
  • We process other participants' data on your behalf — solely to provide the service to you (real-time cues, transcripts, summaries, coaching). We do not use it for any independent purpose, and we never contact the other participants.
  • Deletion cascades. When you delete a session or your account, the other participants' transcript data in it is deleted too.
  • If you were a participant in a conversation transcribed by a wingscript user and want that data removed, contact support@wingscript.com and we will help.

5. How We Use Your Data

  • Generate real-time cue cards during your conversations
  • Build a learning profile to improve coaching over time
  • Provide session summaries and post-call analysis
  • Authenticate you and manage your account
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions
  • Understand product usage and improve the service (content-free analytics only)
  • Secure the service and prevent abuse

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising. We do not train AI models on your data.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Deepgram — real-time speech-to-text. Session audio is streamed to Deepgram for transcription and is not retained by us afterward.
  • Azure OpenAI — processes transcripts for reasoning and cue generation. Data is processed in the US and is not used by Microsoft to train models.
  • Twilio — telephony carrier for calls placed through wingscript's dialing feature.
  • Stripe — payment processing. Stripe collects your payment details directly; we never see full card numbers.
  • Mixpanel — product usage analytics. Receives content-free usage events and account profile information, never conversation content.
  • Nango — OAuth proxy for connectors (Salesforce, HubSpot, Google Drive, Notion, Dialpad). Nango does not store your connected-service data.
  • Google APIs — authentication, read-only calendar access, and exporting your call data to a Google Sheet in your Drive (via the drive.file scope, limited to files wingscript creates) over OAuth 2.0.
  • Apollo.io — contact enrichment for meeting attendee context. Only name, title, and company are retrieved.
  • Google Drive — document sync via Nango for knowledge base content used in meeting preparation.
  • Notion — document sync via Nango for knowledge base content used in meeting preparation.
  • Resend — email delivery. Sends demo-request notifications from our website to our inbox.

These providers are permitted to use your data only to provide their services to us.

8. Data Storage & Retention

  • Data is stored in Azure PostgreSQL in US regions and encrypted at rest.
  • Session transcripts and cue history are retained for your account history. You can delete them at any time.
  • Calendar data is cached temporarily and refreshed periodically.
  • Audio is never stored — it exists only in transient buffers during transcription and is deleted immediately afterward.
  • When you delete your account, your Personal Data is removed from our production systems. Content-free usage records are unlinked from your identity rather than deleted. Residual copies in encrypted backups expire on a fixed rotation schedule.

Some browsers can send "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signals. Because we do not sell or share personal information and do not serve targeted advertising, there is no sale or tracking to opt out of, and we do not change our data collection in response to these signals.

11. Cookies & Local Storage

  • Our applications use browser storage for essential purposes only: keeping you signed in and saving your settings.

12. Security

  • All data in transit is encrypted via TLS; data at rest is encrypted.
  • Audio is never stored — it is transcribed in real time and discarded.
  • Secrets are managed via Azure Key Vault with managed identity access.
  • Sessions use short-lived, signed authentication tokens.
  • If a security breach affects your Personal Data, we will notify you as required by applicable law, including California Civil Code §1798.82.
